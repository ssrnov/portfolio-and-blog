Privacy Policy

Folioryn is designed with a fundamental principle: you own your career narrative and your data. We do not monetize your data, run invasive advertising trackers, or share your academic records without your consent.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for a Folioryn account, we collect your email address, username, and account credentials. As you complete your profile, you may optionally provide: full name, headline, bio, education history (institution, degree, CGPA, graduation dates), skills, project details, employment experience, and social profiles.

2. Public vs. Private Content

Folioryn maintains strict architectural boundaries between draft content and published content:

  • Published Content: Only items you explicitly designate as visible will be rendered on your public /u/username portfolio.
  • Drafts & Private Fields: Unpublished drafts, private contact coordinates (like phone numbers and internal account emails), and unverified notes are guarded by server-enforced authentication policies.
  • Academic Scores: Your CGPA, GPA, or percentage marks will only appear on your live site if you check "Display on Public Portfolio".

3. GitHub & Third-Party OAuth

When you connect your GitHub account, we request only standard read permissions for public repositories. Access tokens are stored with server-side encryption and are never exposed in browser JavaScript. We do not inspect private repositories without your explicit permission.

4. Zero Tracking Cookies & Privacy Analytics

Our analytics are privacy-conscious. We count aggregate page views and referrer domains to show you how many people view your portfolio. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or sell visitor browsing profiles to data brokers.

5. Data Export & Account Deletion

You can export your complete profile in standard JSON format at any time. If you decide to close your account, all associated database records, portfolio drafts, and uploaded media are permanently purged from our active systems.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data protection requests regarding this policy, reach out to our team at buildlab.dev/contact.
